# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private documents, and personal attacks are not accepted.

Security reports and files that may contain personal data must use private reporting channels. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users.

Assume good intent, critique code rather than people, and make space for contributors with different levels of experience and English fluency.
